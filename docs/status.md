# Status

Each feature is listed as built, mocked, planned or cut, with the tracks it serves.

- Built: records how it was verified, as the flow, the result, the date and the commit.
- Mocked: says what is faked and what the real version would need.
- Cut: keeps its line, with the reason.

- Planned: Revolut mispricing check with real data. Recomputes the source's airport figures from published prices, audience counts and attention studies, with attention seconds and the Meta price as sliders. Serves G-Research.

Ranks UK and EU airport placements by how well each airport's route and airline mix matches the startup's target customer, with published or estimated prices and a sent enquiry; two startups are compared side by side in the demo. Serves G-Research and Wordsmith.
