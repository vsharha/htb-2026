# Room Radar: the real cheapest room for your event

Status: proposed, 7 November 2026.

## Idea

A student society or community group describes its event, such as "60 people, Thursday evening, £200, step-free, serving alcohol". An agent searches Tagvenue, council rooms and university rooms. It reads each shortlisted venue's terms for hidden costs such as VAT, booking commission, staff charges, required caterers and cleaning, and checks whether the event needs a licence. It ranks rooms by real cost per attendee against travel time and accessibility, then fills the booking form and stops before submitting.

## Origin

A rework of Daniel's [off-peak venue hire](off-peak-venue-hire.md) idea, made by its advocate agent in a brainstorm on 7 November 2026 at macmac's request. It changes who the idea serves, from startups booking launch venues to societies and community groups. It also changes the focus, from off-peak prices to real cost and hidden free rooms. A judge agent ranked it third (see [idea-ranking.md](../idea-ranking.md)).

## Tracks it could serve

- Wordsmith: strong. One task runs across many sites, then reviews booking terms, which is everyday in-house legal work, and checks licensing.
- G-Research: moderate to strong. The hidden thing is free and idle rooms, and the gap between headline and real cost. It is found from public price lists and the terms, and it lets groups book rooms that would otherwise sit empty.
- Bank of Scotland: moderate. It weighs cost against attendees' travel time, accessibility and turnout, but it is a smaller problem than rent.
- HRT: weak. A fair-price model over the listings is possible, but there is no price history.

## Challenges it could use

- Gemini API: reading venue pages and terms.

## Open problems

These come from agents' web checks on 7 November 2026.

- Tagvenue lists 431 Edinburgh rooms, most with public "from" prices, and loaded without a bot wall. The council publishes library room rates of £17–30 an hour for community groups and £50–83 for commercial hire. The University publishes charitable room rates.
- The University's "Community Access to Rooms" scheme gives local groups rooms free on weekday evenings. It also offers the Nucleus Building and Old College free on weekends from 10 to 4. Only about 9,000 hours have been booked, by about 200 groups.
- Hire Space and many venues show no prices, and community centres have no central price list.
- There is no price or availability history.
- Sending real enquiries to many venues is spam, so the agent stops at submit.
- When an event needs an occasional licence or a public entertainment licence is unverified.
- The problem is small, and judges may find it trivial apart from the free-room moment.

## Scope

Type an event brief. Live browser panes go through about eight sites. The result reads: "Headline £120, real cost £310. Nucleus lecture theatre free on Sunday, you're eligible, form filled, waiting for your submit." The room the judges sit in is free to hire.
